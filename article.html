Are Honey 1, 2 and 3 the same place?

Not according to their listings. Honey2 and Grand Honey3 name different roads, and Honey 1 doesn’t name a road at all. Similar names don’t prove a shared entrance, owner or branch order.

💡 Before you get in a taxi, ask the venue for its full name and a map pin — then check the pin matches the listing.

How to use Honey 1’s plus code

Type WVJM+CMP, Pattaya into Google Maps and compare the pin with the Honey 1 listing. Don’t turn the code into a road name or assume it’s on South Pattaya Road or Second Road. If the pin and the name disagree, message the venue for its current address.

Is the Bangkok “Honey” the same chain?

No. Honey Bee is on Sukhumvit 22 and Honey is on Sukhumvit 33 — both in Bangkok. Filter by city first, then match the full name and address.

Before you go

  • Say the full name: “Grand Honey3”, not “Honey”.
  • Share the map pin with your driver, not the name.
  • Check today’s hours on the listing — they change on holidays.
  • Say “SwanPass” at the counter if the venue has a deal.
