3. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To run the site and your account — contract.
  • To keep the site safe: preventing spam, fraud, abuse and illegal content — legitimate interest and legal obligation.
  • To send alerts you switched on (new deals, new venues) — consent; you can turn each one off in Account → Alerts.
  • To understand how the site is used with analytics cookies — consent (see Cookies).
  • To show venue owners totals (views, clicks, number of messages) — legitimate interest. Owners never see who you are from these numbers.

  • Service providers who host and run the site for us ([hosting provider], Cloudflare, [email provider], [analytics provider]) under data-processing terms.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

We don’t sell your personal data. Some providers may process data outside Thailand; where they do, we use safeguards required by the PDPA.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to access, correct, download (“data portability”), delete, or restrict use of your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself in your account: Account → Preferences has Download my data and Delete my account. For anything else, contact us — we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

7. Deleting your account

Go to Account → Preferences → Delete my account. We delete your account, lists, notes, check-ins, alerts and messages within 30 days (backups roll off within a further 30 days). Your public reviews, questions and tips are deleted too, unless you choose to keep them under “Deleted user”. You can also ask us to delete your data by LINE or email.
